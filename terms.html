Explore Titanic is an independent project: a walkable reconstruction of RMS Titanic that runs in your web browser. It is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by White Star Line, Harland & Wolff, RMS Titanic, Inc., or any museum, archive or other holder of rights in the ship, her plans or her name. "Titanic" and "White Star Line" are used only to name the historic ship and her owners.

Privacy

The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking cookies. Your settings are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device and are never sent anywhere.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (github.io); GitHub's own privacy statement covers its server logs. Fonts, model decoders and the optional live-conversation model are served from this site's own origin. Other servers the page fetches from: none. Conversations run on your computer.
