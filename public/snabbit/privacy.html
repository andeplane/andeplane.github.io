Snabbit Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Snabbit is a single-player arcade game for iPhone and iPad.

Data collection

Snabbit does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. All game progress, scores, and settings are stored only on your device.

Game Center

If you choose to use Game Center, scores are submitted to Apple's Game Center service under your Game Center account. That data is handled by Apple according to Apple's Privacy Policy. Snabbit never sees your Game Center credentials and does not store Game Center data outside your device.

Children

Snabbit does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised at https://github.com/andeplane/snakerace/issues.
