Snake Race Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Snake Race is a single-player arcade game for iPhone and iPad.

Data collection

Starting with version 2.1, Snake Race sends app usage and gameplay events to Mixpanel to help us understand how the game is used and improve it. Events include app opens, session and gameplay duration, worlds played, scores, game outcomes, tutorial use, and changes to sound and control settings. App version, operating system version, and device family are included.

Events are associated with a randomly generated identifier stored by this app on your device. This identifier allows events from the same installation to be connected. Analytics do not include your name, email address, Game Center identity, advertising identifier, vendor identifier, saved-game profile, or turn recordings. IP-derived geolocation is disabled. There are no ads, advertising tracking, session recordings, or cross-app identity matching.

Events are queued on your device and sent over HTTPS. Unsent events expire after five days and the local queue is limited to 500 events. Events received by Mixpanel are retained for analytics; the local queue limits do not describe server retention. For information about the service, see Mixpanel's Privacy Policy. Earlier App Store versions do not include this analytics collection.

No app account is required. Game progress, scores, and settings are stored on your device, with optional Game Center features described below.

Game Center

If you choose to use Game Center, scores and achievement progress are submitted to Apple's Game Center service under your Game Center account. Leaderboards display player names, scores, and ranks supplied by Apple. Game Center identity is used locally to keep each player's progress and pending submissions separate and is not sent to Mixpanel. Apple handles its service data according to Apple's Privacy Policy. Snake Race never sees your Game Center credentials.

Children

Snake Race does not ask for a player's age or personal contact details. The usage analytics described above apply to version 2.1 and later.

Contact
