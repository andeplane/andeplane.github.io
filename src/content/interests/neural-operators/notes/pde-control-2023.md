# Neural Operators for PDE Backstepping Control of First-Order Hyperbolic PIDE with Recycle and Delay

**Authors:** Qi, Jie; Zhang, Jing; Krstic, Miroslav
**First posted / published:** 2023/07/21
**Last revision shown by source:** 2024/06/14
**Publication status:** arXiv version collected; separate publication status not independently verified
**Review depth:** abstract_screened
**Reading location:** Abstract and primary bibliographic record.

Approximates a controller-design operator with DeepONet for a hyperbolic system with recycle and delay.

A genuine operator-learning/control connection. Controller-kernel approximation differs from learning an unconstrained robot world model, and stability arguments depend on the stated system assumptions.
