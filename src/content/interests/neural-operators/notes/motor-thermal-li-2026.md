# Neural Operator–Based Thermal Modeling for Electric Motor and Power Electronics Systems

**Authors:** Jinghao Li

**Publication:** Chalmers master thesis, Systems, Control and Mechatronics, 2026

Studies load-memory-enhanced FNO prediction of two-dimensional transient temperature fields in a 15 kW induction motor.

Master thesis prior art: motor thermal FNO itself is not new. FEM data are placed on a common 512x512 grid; variable sparse sensor observation is a separate proposed question. Few load trajectories limit broad extrapolation claims.
