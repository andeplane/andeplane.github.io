# Identification of Sparse Linear Operators

**Authors:** Heckel, Reinhard; Bölcskei, Helmut
**First posted / published:** 2012/09/24
**Last revision shown by source:** 2013/08/28
**Publication status:** arXiv version collected; separate publication status not independently verified
**Review depth:** abstract_screened
**Reading location:** Abstract and primary bibliographic record.

Identifies structured linear operators from probing signals under conditions on their spreading-function support.

Direct predecessor for learning the operator itself through sparse recovery. The specific time-frequency operator class matters; its guarantees do not cover arbitrary temperature-conditioned kernels.
