# Geometry-Informed Neural Operator for Large-Scale 3D PDEs

**Authors:** Li, Zongyi; Kovachki, Nikola Borislavov; Choy, Chris; Li, Boyi; Kossaifi, Jean; Otta, Shourya Prakash; Nabian, Mohammad Amin; Stadler, Maximilian; Hundt, Christian; Azizzadenesheli, Kamyar; Anandkumar, Anima

Combines graph-based geometry handling with a regular latent Fourier grid for 3D aerodynamic prediction.

Strong example beyond weather and a baseline for irregular geometry. Geometry transfer is tested over a dataset family, not guaranteed across arbitrary equipment.
