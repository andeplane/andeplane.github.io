# Theoretical Perspectives on Deep Learning Methods in Inverse Problems

**Authors:** Jonathan Scarlett; Reinhard Heckel; Miguel R. D. Rodrigues; Paul Hand; Yonina C. Eldar

**Publication:** IEEE JSAIT 3(3):433–453, issue year 2022; revised 2023; DOI 10.1109/JSAIT.2023.3241123

Surveys theoretical guarantees and open problems for generative priors, untrained neural priors, and unfolded reconstruction algorithms.

## Relevance and limits

Explains the evolution of compressed sensing toward learned priors and reconstruction networks. Adjacent background, not a neural-operator paper. Separates signal-model assumptions, optimization, sample complexity and generalization.

**Review:** Primary abstract, relevant method sections and cited reference entries checked; experiments not independently reproduced.
