# Representation Equivalent Neural Operators: a Framework for Alias-free Operator Learning

**Authors:** Francesca Bartolucci; Emmanuel de Bézenac; Bogdan Raonić; Roberto Molinaro; Siddhartha Mishra; Rima Alaifari

**First posted:** 2023. **Downloaded revision:** v2 (2023-11-02).

Introduces representation equivalence and operator aliasing to analyze consistency between continuous operators and their discrete implementations.

## Why it matters

Explains why shared weights across grids do not alone guarantee the same field-level computation. Varying training grids can encourage robustness, but cannot by itself guarantee alias-free evaluation or recover unmeasured information.
