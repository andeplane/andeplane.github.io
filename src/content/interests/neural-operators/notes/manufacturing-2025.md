# Real-time distortion prediction in metallic additive manufacturing via a physics-informed neural operator approach

**Authors:** Tian, Mingxuan; Mu, Haochen; Ding, Donghong; Li, Mengjiao; Ding, Yuhan; Zhao, Jianping
**First posted / published:** 2025/11/17
**Last revision shown by source:** 2025/11/17
**Publication status:** arXiv version collected; separate publication status not independently verified
**Review depth:** targeted full text
**Reading location:** Abstract and FEM-data provenance.

Combines operator and recurrent components to predict distortion from thermal history, with physics-based training constraints.

An industrial time-history example. The training/testing data come from an experimentally validated FEM generator, which is weaker evidence than independent end-to-end production deployment.
