# When Differentiable Programming Meets Spectral PDE Solver

**Authors:** Qijia Jiang

**Publication:** D3S3 workshop at NeurIPS 2024; author-hosted workshop paper

Proposes sparse random-feature encoders recovered by Lasso, learned spectral time stepping, and an extension with learned basis parameters.

## Relevance and limits

Direct conceptual bridge to using compressed sensing inside operator learning. Sections 2.2 and 3 allow distinct random input/output grids and recover sparse coefficients before learning dynamics. Physics is built into the spectral evolution. Workshop proposal: large-scale systematic evaluation is explicitly future work; not evidence of a generic training speedup or an unconditional exact-recovery guarantee.

**Review:** Primary abstract, relevant method sections and cited reference entries checked; experiments not independently reproduced.
