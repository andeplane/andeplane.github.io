# Generalizable Motion Planning via Operator Learning

**Authors:** Matada, Sharath; Bhan, Luke; Shi, Yuanyuan; Atanasov, Nikolay
**First posted / published:** 2024/10/23
**Last revision shown by source:** 2025/05/26
**Publication status:** ICLR 2025; inspected PDF states venue
**Review depth:** targeted full text
**Reading location:** Abstract, introduction, manipulator setup and visual first-page verification.

Learns the map from spatial cost functions to motion-planning value functions, motivated by an Eikonal PDE.

Direct robotics evidence, including 4-DOF manipulation planning. It predicts a planning quantity rather than action-conditioned physical evolution of manipulated objects.
