# Multiwavelet-based Operator Learning for Differential Equations

**Authors:** Gaurav Gupta, Xiongye Xiao, Paul Bogdan

**First preprint:** 2021 · **Review depth:** abstract and bibliographic record, checked 2026-09-10.

Projects operator kernels onto fixed multiwavelet polynomial bases and learns interactions across scales. Demonstrated on several PDE families.

## Why read it?

Compare a localized, multiscale representation with the global Fourier representation in the teaching labs. Distinguish the fixed transform from the learned operator acting through it.

## Limits and application questions

Kernel compression here is a multiscale representation, not a compressed-sensing recovery guarantee. The abstract does not establish embedded vibration-monitoring latency.
