# Wavelet neural operator: a neural operator for parametric partial differential equations

**Authors:** Tapas Tripura, Souvik Chakraborty

**First preprint:** 2022 · **Review depth:** abstract and bibliographic record, checked 2026-09-10.

Combines learned integral-kernel operations with localized wavelet transforms. Examples include PDE solution maps and air-temperature prediction.

## Why read it?

Compare a localized, multiscale representation with the global Fourier representation in the teaching labs. Distinguish the fixed transform from the learned operator acting through it.

## Limits and application questions

Spatial/frequency localization motivates studying transient signals, but this paper is not evidence of millisecond fault detection inside an IMU. Validate sampling, window length, memory and end-to-end delay separately.
