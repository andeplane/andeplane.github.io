# The Parametric Complexity of Operator Learning

**Authors:** Samuel Lanthaler; Andrew M. Stuart

**First posted:** 2023. **Downloaded revision:** v4 (2025-03-09).

Proves a curse of parametric complexity for general regular operator classes, and shows how Hamilton–Jacobi structure permits a more efficient architecture.

## Why it matters

Addresses whether operator models can be smaller: no universal compression law follows from using an operator architecture. Efficiency depends on structure in the target operator. These are approximation-complexity results, not empirical training scaling laws.

**Review depth:** Abstract and reference list checked; numerical results not independently reproduced.
