# CECM: A continuous empirical cubature method with application to the dimensional hyperreduction of parameterized finite element models

**Authors:** Hernandez, J. A.; Bravo, J. R.; de Parga, S. Ares
**First posted / published:** 2023/08/07
**Last revision shown by source:** 2023/08/07
**Publication status:** arXiv version collected; separate publication status not independently verified
**Review depth:** targeted full text
**Reading location:** Abstract and two-stage cubature description.

Optimizes integration locations as well as weights to obtain small positive cubature rules.

Very close to smart-point selection. It assumes evaluations at movable points are available; fixed physical sensors impose a different feasible set.
