# Discovering governing equations from data: Sparse identification of nonlinear dynamical systems

**Authors:** Brunton, Steven L.; Proctor, Joshua L.; Kutz, J. Nathan
**First posted / published:** 2015/09/11
**Last revision shown by source:** 2015/09/11
**Publication status:** arXiv version collected; separate publication status not independently verified
**Review depth:** abstract_screened
**Reading location:** Abstract and primary bibliographic record.

Fits sparse combinations of candidate nonlinear functions to discover dynamical equations from data.

Useful sparse-regression comparator. Learning an evolution equation differs from learning a finite-horizon solution operator; derivative estimation and the dictionary are consequential.
