# DeepVIVONet: Using deep neural operators to optimize sensor locations with application to vortex-induced vibrations

**Authors:** Wan, Ruyin; Kharazmi, Ehsan; Triantafyllou, Michael S; Karniadakis, George Em
**First posted / published:** 2025/01/07
**Last revision shown by source:** 2025/01/07
**Publication status:** arXiv version collected; separate publication status not independently verified
**Review depth:** targeted_full_text
**Reading location:** Abstract, sensor-optimization discussion and Section 5 conclusions.

Uses marine-riser field data for sparse reconstruction and forecasting, then optimizes sensor placement with an outer loop.

Closest applied match to industrial sensing. Transfer to different flow conditions involves transfer learning; optimized physical sensors are not the same as cheap post-acquisition quadrature subsampling.
