# U-FNO -- An enhanced Fourier neural operator-based deep-learning model for multiphase flow

**Authors:** Wen, Gege; Li, Zongyi; Azizzadenesheli, Kamyar; Anandkumar, Anima; Benson, Sally M.
**First posted / published:** 2021/09/03
**Last revision shown by source:** 2022/05/04
**Publication status:** arXiv version collected; separate publication status not independently verified
**Review depth:** abstract screened
**Reading location:** Abstract and primary bibliographic record.

Combines U-Net-style processing with Fourier operator components for multiphase subsurface flow.

A concrete industrially relevant application: pressure and saturation fields during CO2 injection. Validation is for the modeled parameter family, not unrestricted reservoir transfer.
