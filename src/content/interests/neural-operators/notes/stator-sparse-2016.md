# Sparse Reconstruction for Temperature Distribution Using DTS Fiber Optic Sensors with Applications in Electrical Generator Stator Monitoring

**Authors:** João Paulo Bazzo; Daniel Rodrigues Pipa; Erlon Vagner da Silva; Cicero Martelli; Jean Carlos Cardozo da Silva

**Publication:** Sensors 16(9), 1425 (2016); DOI 10.3390/s16091425

Uses a finite-element hotspot dictionary and sparse reconstruction with distributed fibre temperatures to monitor generator stators.

A direct industrial sparse-recovery baseline, not a neural operator. Experiments use a representative prototype; no reusable dataset was verified. Useful motivation for a thermal-plate autumn study and stator extension.

[Primary source](https://doi.org/10.3390/s16091425)

**Review:** Primary abstract/bibliographic screening, 14 September 2026. No numerical results independently reproduced.
