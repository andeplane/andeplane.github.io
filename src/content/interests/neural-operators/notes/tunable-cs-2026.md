# Latent Generative Models with Tunable Complexity for Compressed Sensing and other Inverse Problems

**Authors:** Sean Gunn; Jorio Cocola; Oliver De Candido; Vaggos Chatziafratis; Paul Hand

**Publication:** arXiv preprint, March 2026, v2

Uses nested dropout to vary latent-prior complexity in diffusion models, normalizing flows and variational autoencoders for inverse problems.

## Relevance and limits

Current compressed-sensing direction: tune prior complexity to balance representation error and noise overfitting. Adjacent to operator learning, not a neural-operator architecture. March 2026 preprint; general experiments and theory for the linear denoising setting do not establish universal compressed-sensing guarantees.

**Review:** Primary abstract, relevant method sections and cited reference entries checked; experiments not independently reproduced.
