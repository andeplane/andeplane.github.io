# A neural operator-based surrogate solver for free-form electromagnetic inverse design

**Authors:** Augenstein, Yannick; Repän, Taavi; Rockstuhl, Carsten
**First posted / published:** 2023/02/04
**Last revision shown by source:** 2023/03/28
**Publication status:** arXiv version collected; separate publication status not independently verified
**Review depth:** abstract_screened
**Reading location:** Abstract and primary bibliographic record.

Uses a neural-operator surrogate for repeated electromagnetic evaluation during inverse design.

Illustrates the many-query business case. Final designs should be checked using the original physical solver, since an optimizer may exploit surrogate error.
