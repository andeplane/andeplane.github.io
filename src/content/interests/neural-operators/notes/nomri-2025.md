# A Unified Model for Compressed Sensing MRI Across Undersampling Patterns

**Authors:** Armeet Singh Jatyani; Jiayun Wang; Aditi Chandrashekar; Zihui Wu; Miguel Liu-Schiaffini; Bahareh Tolooshams; Anima Anandkumar

**Publication:** CVPR 2025; arXiv first posted 2024

Uses neural operators in measurement and image space with data-consistency updates to reconstruct MRI across undersampling patterns and resolutions.

## Relevance and limits

Direct neural-operator application to compressed-sensing reconstruction. Learns a prior for undersampled MRI; it does not use sparse recovery to make arbitrary kernel training faster. CVPR 2025 experiments, not a clinical deployment claim.

**Review:** Primary abstract, relevant method sections and cited reference entries checked; experiments not independently reproduced.
