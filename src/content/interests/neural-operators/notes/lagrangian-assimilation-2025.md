# Generative Lagrangian data assimilation for ocean dynamics under extreme sparsity

**Authors:** Niloofar Asefi; Leonard Lupin-Jimenez; Tianning Wu; Ruoying He; Ashesh Chattopadhyay

**Publication:** arXiv preprint, July 2025

Combines neural operators and diffusion models for ocean-state reconstruction from very sparse Lagrangian observations.

Prior art for moving-sensor reconstruction; a controlled active-sampling study on 2D passive tracer dynamics is a narrower potential thesis. Sparse reconstructions depend on learned priors and are not unique recovery guarantees.
