Uses multiscale kernel-frame representations of input and output functions for operator learning.

Late-August 2026 preprint offering a structured coefficient-space alternative to standard neural operators. A potentially useful comparator for sparse-basis ideas, but multiscale frames do not automatically imply sparse recoverability or cheap sensor acquisition.
