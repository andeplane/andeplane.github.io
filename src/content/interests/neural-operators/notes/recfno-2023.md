# RecFNO: a resolution-invariant flow and heat field reconstruction method from sparse observations via Fourier neural operator

**Authors:** Xiaoyu Zhao; Xiaoqian Chen; Zhiqiang Gong; Weien Zhou; Wen Yao; Yunyang Zhang

**Publication:** International Journal of Thermal Sciences 195 (2024), 108619; DOI 10.1016/j.ijthermalsci.2023.108619

Reconstructs heat and flow fields from sparse observations using MLP, mask or Voronoi embeddings and Fourier layers.

Direct prior art for sparse thermal reconstruction. Output resolution transfer is distinct from generalization to arbitrary input layouts. Public code: https://github.com/zhaoxiaoyu1995/RecFNO ; data links are external SharePoint links and access has not been tested.

[Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.09808)

**Review:** Primary abstract/bibliographic screening, 14 September 2026. No numerical results independently reproduced.
