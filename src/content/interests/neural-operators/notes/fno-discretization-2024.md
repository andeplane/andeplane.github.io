# Discretization Error of Fourier Neural Operators

**Authors:** Lanthaler, Samuel; Stuart, Andrew M.; Trautner, Margaret
**First posted / published:** 2024/05/03
**Last revision shown by source:** 2025/09/25
**Publication status:** arXiv version collected; separate publication status not independently verified
**Review depth:** targeted_full_text
**Reading location:** Abstract and error framing.

Analyzes errors introduced when a continuous Fourier neural operator is evaluated on a finite grid.

Essential for the inference-quadrature thesis: accurately fitting training outputs does not determine how aggressively evaluation grids can be reduced.
