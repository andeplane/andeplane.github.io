# People and research communities

A selective map of communities relevant to this collection, checked against primary institutional/project pages on 10 September 2026. These are starting points for following work, not lab rankings or claims about available supervision. Paper authorship is recorded separately in the graph; it does not imply a current affiliation.

| Community / people | Why follow them for this project? | Primary starting point |
|---|---|---|
| Ben Adcock, Simon Fraser University | Sparse approximation, compressed sensing and sampling theory; connects directly to the 2017 seed and later operator-sample-complexity work. | [SFU profile](https://www.sfu.ca/math/people/faculty/adcockb.html) |
| Simone Brugiapaglia, Concordia University | High-dimensional approximation and mathematical learning theory; coauthor of the seed and the 2026 operator-learning theory tour. | [Concordia profile](https://www.concordia.ca/faculty/simone-brugiapaglia.html) |
| Anima Anandkumar and collaborators, Caltech | Neural operator architectures, scientific applications and reusable implementations; several seed and application papers share this author network. | [Caltech neural-operator project](https://www.sase.caltech.edu/projects/neuraloperators.html) |
| Andrew Stuart, Caltech | Mathematical foundations of learning maps between function spaces, scientific computing and inverse problems. | [Caltech profile](https://www.cms.caltech.edu/people/astuart) |
| Siddhartha Mishra and collaborators, ETH Zurich | Operator learning, approximation theory, inverse operators and scientific foundation models; see NIO and Poseidon. | [Computational and Applied Mathematics Laboratory](https://camlab.ethz.ch/the-group/people.html?u=smishra) |
| George Karniadakis, Brown University | DeepONet and physics-informed scientific learning; industrial and biomedical applications in the collection. | [Brown profile](https://engineering.brown.edu/people/george-e-karniadakis) |
| Lu Lu, Yale University | Deep operator learning and scientific machine learning, including the original DeepONet thread. | [Yale profile](https://wti.yale.edu/profile/lu-lu) |
| Yuanyuan Shi and collaborators, UC San Diego | A direct operator-learning/control intersection; follow the planning-neural-operator work. | [Operator learning for control](https://yyshi.eng.ucsd.edu/research/operator-learning-for-control) |
| Varun Shankar and collaborators, University of Utah | Kernel methods, numerical approximation and quadrature-aware operator learning; KNO and frame kernels are useful for the numerical-methods branch. | [Utah profile](https://www.math.utah.edu/directory/people-info/shankar.php) |
| Empirical-cubature authors: J. A. Hernández and collaborators | Selected-point evaluation of nonlinear reduced models, moving nodes and state-dependent weights; an essential neighboring numerical community. | [CECM paper and author list](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.03877), [adaptive cubature](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03068) |
| Soft-robotics authors: Christian Veil, Moritz Flaschel, Ellen Kuhl, Cosimo Della Santina | Differentiable actuation-to-shape operators and inverse kinematics. Follow the paper's group links for this specific bridge. | [Soft-robot paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.18655) |
| Meta's V-JEPA research community | Video representations and action-conditioned world models; an adjacent perspective for robotics, not the same architecture family as neural operators. | [V-JEPA project](https://ai.meta.com/research/vjepa/) |

For a numerics-oriented thesis, the Adcock/Brugiapaglia and Shankar/cubature reading threads are particularly complementary. For a robotics-oriented thesis, compare Shi's planning operators with the soft-robot shape maps and V-JEPA's action-conditioned prediction. These pairings are editorial suggestions based on the papers, not evidence of an existing collaboration.
