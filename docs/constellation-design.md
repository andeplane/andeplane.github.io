# The light we leave — experience and relationship plan

Current specification, 25 October 2026. A generic life addressed as **You**, with
the exact personal dedication to Mikael at the end. The existing 119.97-second
instrumental is unchanged: no lyrics, voice or humming.

## Who moves, and why

- Mom and Dad keep a steady paired course. You leave their cluster in adolescence,
  reach your greatest independence in your twenties, then move closer in your forties.
- Kindergarten friend crosses left to right near You at age three, keeping a constant
  sideways pace. The encounter never turns into a parallel companion track. Its
  trail exists before meeting and continues offscreen afterward.
- Grade school friend crosses in the opposite direction near age eight. Middle
  school friend passes at thirteen. Coworker weaves across your course from 22
  to 25.5, then leaves. These are short encounters, not permanent companion lanes.
- Childhood friend approaches from an existing life outside the right edge. You
  travel very close through childhood. The friendship grows more distant as college
  friendship becomes important, then continues out of view without a death marker.
- First love approaches from the left and stays closer to You than to either friend.
  Their departure keeps moving in time; it never falls behind as a dead person's endpoint.
- College friend approaches from the left, becomes a close companion in the twenties,
  and gradually leaves during early parenthood. The two friendships trade prominence.
- Girlfriend or Boyfriend approaches from an existing life at 26, becomes Wife or
  Husband at 29, and stays adjacent. The partner dies at 82, before You at 85.
  Children arrive at 30 and 32. Parenting spreads
  the household; the couple gradually finds closeness again.
- Children move with the household while young. Their own paths bend outward as
  teenagers and adults; their parents are not pushed away to manufacture that distance.
  The son draws closer while raising his own young family, then makes a distinct
  outward bend around your ages 68–77 while the older couple keeps its course.
- Brother is two years older and dies at 40, when You are 38. No birth or new encounter
  competes with that moment. His light holds briefly, other paths dim, then his quiet
  star joins the remembered lives. The rest of the family continues.
- Sister is born when You are eight and remains on the original-family side.

At a death, the final six years of the path peel left and stop. The finished
trail stays in the past; a separate light rises over five seconds into the sky.
Brother has an additional 1.8-second held moment. There is no flash or expanding circle;
a steady, small glowing cross remains, with a quieter version of its original hue.
You stays on your own course until death, becoming the final remembered star.

Names have fixed tiers and bounded eased corrections. Vertical gaps do not trigger
unnecessary horizontal separation: You and companions keep their names near their
own dots. Living paths keep their colors; remembered lives keep a restrained hint of their own color.

## Ending and continuing time

You die at 85, 90 seconds into the score. The surviving family continues in 2D.
The remembered stars draw an original, asymmetric seven-star arch from 95.5 to
101.5 seconds: You → partner → Mom → Grandma → Grandpa → Dad → Brother → You.
Grandparents form the roof, parents the shoulders, partner and Brother the lower
sides, and You the base. The perimeter does not cross itself. From 102 to 118 seconds
the view lowers to 30 degrees above the time plane. The whole constellation settles
into the foreground, where You stays labelled permanently.

Year 100 arrives at the end of the two-minute score. Only then does time accelerate,
gradually approaching five years per second; the next century takes about 28 seconds.
From years 120–200, individual life paths dissolve into a collective canopy. The
canopy has visible roots in You and your partner and forks into shared generations.
Light moves along those same forks, without individual dots, pulses, or unrelated
particle emitters. Its motion uses seconds so the accelerating year clock cannot
make it frantic. At 200 the individual trail renderer stops; the counter fades
between 200 and 230. A bounded canopy and flowing light continue indefinitely,
with the seven-star constellation and dedication remaining in place.

Curved cubic trails replace polygonal line segments. Encounters approach over
8–9 years and depart over seven. Passing school friends move at 90 world units
per year, down from 200; the coworker makes a smaller, slower weave. The family
is centered around x=440 with grandparents on both sides, using the full canvas.
Glows and label spacing use screen pixels rather than ballooning on wide displays.
