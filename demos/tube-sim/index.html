Tube Acoustics Lab

A real 2D acoustic wave solver. Strike the left end and watch the overpressure pulse travel down the tube, meet a hole, and split into what escapes, what reflects, and what continues.

Excitation

Tube

An open end reflects the pulse back inverted — compression returns as suction. Cap it and the same pulse comes back the way it left. Drop a meter mid-tube and watch the sign of the returning peak.

Holes

Drag the middle of a hole on the tube to move it, or drag its edge to resize it. A hole is a real gap in the wall — its size changes the physics, not the drawing.

Playback

Scroll or pinch to zoom in on the hole; drag the air to pan. Space strikes, P pauses, R resets, 0 resets the view.

Pressure meters

Click anywhere in the air to drop a meter (up to 3), and read p(t) in the plot along the bottom. To remove one, click the meter itself (it turns into an ×) or the × on its chip in the plot header.
