The Hall of Valves

A real language model, run on water. Each weight is a valve between a reservoir pipe and a collector; laminar flow multiplies, the collector adds, and a whole matrix product happens at once. Fill the reservoirs, then zoom from the machine down to a single valve.

The machine

Weights stream straight from Hugging Face into your browser and are kept in its cache.

Physics

Digital, and labelled so: tokenising, layer norm, GELU, softmax, the attention mask and sampling. Everything that multiplies is water.

Explore

Physics: Hagen–Poiseuille valves (flow = conductance × head), Kirchhoff collectors, differential pairs for signed weights, quantised valve detents, manufacturing tolerance, flow-meter noise and first-order manifold IR drop per 64×64 tile.
