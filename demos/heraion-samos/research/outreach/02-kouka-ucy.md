# 2 — Prof. Ourania Kouka (University of Cyprus)

**To:** ouraniak@ucy.ac.cy *(inferred address — see `../MANIFEST.md` §2.4)*
**Subject:** Heraion of Samos — 3D reconstruction and simulation model of the prehistoric settlement

---

Dear Prof. Kouka,

I am developing an augmented-reality experience for visitors to the Heraion of
Samos, and I am writing to you as director of the project on the prehistoric
settlement north of the Sacred Road.

The DAI project page credits two outputs that are directly relevant to what I am
trying to build: the 3D digital reconstruction of the Early Bronze Age settlement
by Angeliki Chalkia, and the simulation model by Markos Panagiotou using Blender,
Autodesk 3ds Max and Unity. I want to be clear that I understand these are models
of the prehistoric settlement rather than of the later temples — my project began
with the Polykrates-era Great Temple, but a visitor standing at the Heraion is
standing on a site with a far longer history than that, and being able to show the
Early Bronze Age settlement would make the experience considerably better than
another temple reconstruction on its own.

I would therefore like to ask whether those files still exist and whether there
is any route to using them. Concretely:

- do the Blender / 3ds Max / Unity project files survive, and with whom do they sit?
- would the project consider making them available for a visitor-facing AR
  application at the site, under conditions you set?
- if so, could I also receive the textures, the scale and georeferencing
  convention, and some record of which parts of the reconstruction are evidenced
  and which are interpretive? I would want to mark uncertainty honestly in the
  app rather than present a hypothesis as fact.
- what credit line would you and your colleagues require?

If the files cannot be shared, I would still be very grateful for two much smaller
things. First, whether Ms. Chalkia and Mr. Panagiotou would be willing to be
contacted directly — I would rather approach them through you than around you.
Second, whether you know of any digital reconstruction of the Great Temple itself,
by your team or anyone else; that is the one thing I have been unable to establish
from the published record, and a one-line answer either way would save me a good
deal of guesswork.

I should mention that the intended outcome is a visitor-facing application, and I
do not yet know whether it will be freely distributed or operated commercially. I
would rather flag that at the outset than seek revised permissions later.

I have written separately to Dr. Henke at the DAI regarding the sanctuary
excavation, so you may hear of this from both directions.
