# 1 — Dr. Jan-Marc Henke (DAI Athens)

**To:** Jan-Marc.Henke@dainst.de
**Subject:** Heraion of Samos — enquiry on digital documentation of the Great Temple for a visitor AR project

---

Dear Dr. Henke,

I am developing an augmented-reality experience for visitors to the Heraion of
Samos, centred on the Polykrates-era Great Temple, and I am writing to you as
director of the DAI excavation project at the sanctuary.

Before I start building anything, I would like to know what already exists. My
question is therefore quite specific: does the excavation project hold, or know
of, any digital three-dimensional documentation of the sanctuary — laser scans,
photogrammetric meshes, survey point clouds, CAD or SketchUp models, or a
reconstruction of Dipteros I or II in any form? I am aware of the 3D
reconstruction and simulation work carried out for the Early Bronze Age settlement
north of the Sacred Road, and I have written separately to Prof. Kouka about that;
what I have not been able to establish from the published record is whether any
comparable material exists for the temple itself.

If such material does exist and could be made available, I would want to discuss:

- native or exchange-format files (.blend, .max, .fbx, .obj, .gltf, .dwg, .las/.laz)
- textures and materials, where they exist
- the georeferencing and scale convention used, so I can align to the extant remains
- documentation of which parts are measured and which are reconstructed hypothesis
- the terms under which a derived, optimised AR asset (GLB/USDZ) could be shown to
  visitors, and the credit line you would require

If nothing of the sort exists, or it cannot be shared, then a single sentence
saying so would already be very useful to me — it tells me to plan for a
reconstruction built from the published plans instead, which is a materially
different budget and timeline.

In that case I would also be grateful for your view on one narrower question:
for a reconstruction of the Great Temple, which published plans would you regard
as the authoritative geometric basis today? I have been working from Samos 21.1
and the plan sheets on iDAI.publications, but I would rather build on what the
project considers current than on what I happen to have found.

I should say plainly that the intended outcome is a visitor-facing application. I
do not yet know whether it will be freely distributed or operated on a commercial
or sponsored basis, and I would rather raise that now than ask you to revisit
permissions later. Any DAI material used would of course be credited as you
specify, and I would not publish redrawings of DAI plans without written
permission.

With thanks for your time, and with respect for the project's work,
