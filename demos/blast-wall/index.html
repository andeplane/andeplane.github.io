WebGPU required

This lab runs a finite element solver over thousands of bricks on your GPU, and needs a browser with WebGPU. Recent Chrome, Edge or Safari should work; in Firefox, enable it in about:config.

Blast Wall Lab

Bricks in løperforband, mortar joints that crack, slide, bear and crush, and a Friedlander pulse arriving from outside. Every joint is solved, not scripted.

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, shift-drag to pan. Click a brick to select it; drag the red charge to move it.
