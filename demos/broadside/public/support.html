Broadside & Captain Calculus

Games by Anders Hafreager for iPhone and iPad.

Game support

For help or to report a problem, contact Anders using the project’s support issues or the contact links on the developer website.

Include the game name, app version, device and iOS version, and what happened. Do not post passwords, payment information or a child’s personal details.

Broadside

Steer with the wheel and tap BOOM to fire. Board your ship to explore the deck and walk through the marked gold-room doorway. Earned chests stay aboard until you return to your cave and unload them.

Captain Calculus

Choose an operator and a difficulty island, then solve ten problems. Each correct first-try answer earns 100 gold. Hints are free; a mistake affects only that problem’s reward. Chests remain aboard until their coins finish unloading in the cave.

Progress and purchases

Progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app removes its local game data. There are no accounts, advertisements or in-app purchases. App Store downloads, billing and refund requests are handled by Apple.
