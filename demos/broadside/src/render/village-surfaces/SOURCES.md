# Village surface scans

The albedo and OpenGL normal maps are derived from Poly Haven scans. The game uses locally bundled WebP files with adjusted exposure for painted materials. The sailcloth texture uses a mirrored crop of the fabric weave. [Poly Haven CC0 license](https://polyhaven.com/license)

| --- | --- |

| Stone lanes | [Cobblestone Floor 08](https://polyhaven.com/a/cobblestone_floor_08) |
| Pantiles | [Clay Roof Tiles 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/clay_roof_tiles_02) |
