# Captain Calculus presentation

This record applies to Captain Calculus only. Broadside and the shared cave/3D environment retain their separate ownership.

Children aged 6–10 playing independently need clear actions, readable arithmetic and an inviting world. Harbour art fills the home screen behind the notch; title/actions receive safe-area padding. A home screen without a header never reserves header space. Other pages have a compact dedicated navigation row.

## Tokens

Ink #0b2840, secondary #415d6a, ivory #fff6e3, background #e7f5f3, ocean #087e96, gold action #f5ba32, focus #075d75. Display: locally hosted Fraunces700; body/controls: DM Sans. Flat reading surfaces, 12–16px corners, 44px minimum controls (normally 52–56px). Functional copy avoids tiny decorative labels.

## Structure

Home art bleeds to all screen edges. Maps reserve title/chapter and action-dock space outside the pan viewport; recenter/zoom stay inside it. The active chapter remains in the header. Questions use one column in portrait, two in short landscape; narrow short screens use the textual question counter instead of duplicate progress dots. Hint diagrams remain whole in normal page flow; the page scrolls to the answer controls. Landscape ship headings and cargo occupy separate areas; an empty ship explains its disabled unloading action.

Eight maintained languages, first-try rewards, per-operation banks, current saves, cargo and full world maps are preserved. New visual CSS is scoped to Calculus. Cave/unloading styles and rendering were deliberately not edited by this work.
