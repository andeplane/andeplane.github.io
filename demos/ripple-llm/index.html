Wave computing · after Hughes et al., 2019

The Ripple LLM

Waves add up linearly, so a tank driven at one frequency turns the strengths of its wave-makers into readings at its probes through a fixed matrix. Sculpt the floor and you choose the matrix.

Twenty-four sculpted tanks hold every weight of a tiny character-level language model. Each letter it writes is a wave pattern crossing all of them.

Each probe feeds a lock-in amplifier: it multiplies the probe's height by the wave-makers' own clock and averages over whole periods. That in-phase amplitude is the signed answer (a negative input is the same stroke half a period late). One fixed gain converts it to numbers, the same for every tank.
